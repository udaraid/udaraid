Where it began

The July–August 2024 Revolution

What started as an anti-discrimination student movement to reform the quota system for government jobs quickly became a battleground as the government attacked its own people. Brave citizens took to the streets demanding justice and freedom, only to be met with violence. In the days leading to victory on August 5th, indiscriminate shooting and arson left hundreds injured — rickshaw pullers and street vendors became amputees from gunshot wounds — and countless families lost their livelihoods.

UDAR AID was born from the ashes of that crisis. What began as an urgent response to help injured protesters — in partnership with CSE-DU, Computer Science and Engineering students from Dhaka University — has grown into a humanitarian organization dedicated to rebuilding lives and restoring dignity.

Past campaigns

Campaign 1 · Medical

Emergency Medical Attention for the Injured

We provided immediate financial assistance for urgent medical care to revolution survivors hospitalized across Bangladesh. Through our partnership with CSE-DU, we maintained direct communication with patients, assessed their needs, and transferred funds for medications, surgeries, and post-treatment care.

Survivors faced a healthcare crisis — limited access to quality treatment and mounting expenses. Many were breadwinners whose injuries left their families without income.

  • 292 patients documented
  • 280 received direct financial support
  • Comprehensive patient database maintained
  • Individual case tracking and follow-up
Campaign 2 · Rehabilitation

Long-Term Rehabilitation Support

From January through June 2025, we committed to sustained monthly support for four severely injured families — a minimum of BDT 10,000 per family each month for food, rent, medical expenses, and children's education while primary earners recover.

Many survivors sustained permanent disabilities requiring long-term care. Without ongoing support, these families faced destitution as medical costs continued while income disappeared.

Featured families

  • Manik & Faruk — father and son injured by petrol bombs, supporting a 7-member family
  • Nupur — shot in both legs, living alone, former salesperson
  • Mamun — shattered hand bones, sole provider for his wife and grandmother
  • Mufti Firoz — 265 rubber bullet wounds, children unable to attend school
Campaign 3 · Relief

Flood Fund Relief

After the catastrophic August 2024 floods in south-eastern Bangladesh, UDAR AID worked in close partnership with Al Asr, a faith-based humanitarian organization, to reach the most affected families in the Noakhali and Feni region — mobilizing resources and deploying teams on the ground for swift distribution of emergency relief.

Tens of thousands were left exposed to hunger and homelessness overnight — communities among the most vulnerable in Bangladesh, often overlooked by mainstream aid.

  • 300+ families received emergency relief packages
  • Food, hygiene kits, clean water, and shelter materials
  • One completely destroyed home rebuilt — a new start for a family that lost everything
Campaign 4 · Food

Ramadan & Eid Campaigns

We organized comprehensive Ramadan and Eid support — iftar meals for orphan children, grocery support for day laborers, and Eid meals for needy families — through partnerships with local organizations.

The post-revolution economic crisis made it hard for vulnerable families to observe Ramadan with dignity. We aimed to ensure economic hardship didn't prevent spiritual observance and community celebration.

  • $1,425 total distributed
  • 200+ families benefited
  • 100+ orphan children supported
Education

Madrassa Project

Supporting a remote village madrassa in Kishoreganj (Itna Upazilla) to build a permanent school structure for 70+ students from low-income families — a safer, more permanent learning space, especially during monsoon season.

  • 98,289 BDT raised at a New York canvas session and sent for construction
  • Construction in full swing; total project need is about 3,20,000 BDT

Individuals in Crisis

Ongoing campaigns

We are providing long-term rehabilitation support to families of the July Revolution, funded largely by Ramadan zakat donations.

Matiur Rahman — Urgent Surgery

Raising support for Matiur's urgent surgery, estimated at 5 lakh BDT.

Mufti Feroz — Back on His Feet

Helping our past sponsorship recipient purchase a laptop so he can complete his technical program and build apps.

Imon — Treatment & Education

Covering ongoing medical treatment and education for 12-year-old Imon, who was shot in the leg during the July protests.

Hatia Flood Relief

Sending emergency aid — food packets, hygiene kits, and shelter support — to families displaced by intense flooding on Hatia Island, Noakhali.

Our first internship program

In June 2025 we launched our first Internship Program in Dhaka, Bangladesh. The pilot became an instant success for both UDAR and its beneficiaries — our ground intern conducts in-person verification of every beneficiary, paving the way for more interns and further growth.

Partnerships that amplify impact

Our partnerships with local organizations like CSE-DU (Dhaka University) and the Al-Asr Foundation ensure we reach the most vulnerable — with detailed documentation of every case, every dollar spent, and every life touched.
